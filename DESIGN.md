# Design System

## Theme

Bright, spacious and kinetic. True white surfaces, black structure, warm orange accents, and large amounts of deliberate negative space. The interface feels like a digital workshop rather than an agency template.

## Color

- Canvas: `#FFFFFF`
- Ink: `#000000` / `#1A1A1A`
- Primary orange: `#FF7E35`
- Orange light: `#FF9A5C`
- Orange dark: `#E66A1A`
- Muted text: `#6B6B6B`
- Border: `#E5E5E5`

Orange may appear as a left-to-right dark-to-light gradient for primary actions. It is used selectively in italic serif words, buttons, and emphasis.

## Typography

- Structural sans: Geist or a neutral grotesk, bold for concise section titles and regular for body copy.
- Expressive display: Instrument Serif, especially italic, for highlighted words and large kinetic text.
- Headings often combine both families on one line: direct black sans followed by an orange serif-italic word.
- Body text is compact, left aligned, and relatively narrow.

## Layout

- White, full-width canvas with long vertical breathing room.
- Content typically aligns to a restrained central column while marquee text and visual experiments can break the viewport.
- Sections are paced as distinct moments instead of being placed in repeated containers.
- Thin horizontal rules structure navigation, forms, and footer content.
- Asymmetry, perspective, blur, oversized typography, and off-grid objects are intentional brand devices.

## Components

- Minimal top navigation with small text links.
- Mixed-type section heading: bold sans + orange serif italic.
- Project objects that use perspective and different visual treatments.
- Oversized serif skill marquee that runs beyond the viewport.
- Underlined form inputs with a strong orange gradient submit button.
- Quiet multi-column footer separated with hairline rules.

## Motion

- Blur-to-sharp text reveals.
- Perspective and hover movement for project objects.
- Continuous horizontal type motion for skills.
- Magnetic interaction on important controls.
- Reduced-motion alternatives must preserve all content and hierarchy.

## Proposal Adaptation

Static documents translate motion through blurred oversized background words, directional type, cropped serif display text, asymmetrical composition, angled route panels, and strong changes in scale. Avoid default corporate cover pages, repeated kicker labels, dense tables, and boxed content on every page.
