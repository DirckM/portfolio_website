# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential clients, collaborators, employers, and other designers or developers who want to understand Dirck Mulder's work, range, and way of thinking quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present Dirck as a multidisciplinary maker who combines product thinking, interface design, and full-stack development. The portfolio should make technical breadth feel human and memorable while leading qualified visitors toward project exploration or direct contact.

## Brand Personality

Curious, crafted, and kinetic. Confident without corporate polish; expressive without visual noise.

## Anti-references

Generic agency templates, dense SaaS card grids, corporate proposal styling, decorative glassmorphism, and interfaces whose branding is limited to an accent color.

## Design Principles

1. Let whitespace create anticipation and focus.
2. Pair direct sans-serif structure with expressive serif moments.
3. Treat motion and unusual composition as evidence of craft.
4. Show range through changing visual moments while keeping the voice coherent.
5. Make important actions and information obvious despite the experimental presentation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable body copy, keyboard-accessible interactions, clear focus states, responsive behavior, and reduced-motion alternatives for expressive animation.
