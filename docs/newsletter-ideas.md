# Newsletter ideas

Posts that start with a question somebody actually asked. Each idea becomes a blog
post first, then goes out in the next newsletter issue with a link to that post.

Inbox → Writing → Live → Sent. Move the bullet down as it progresses.

Entry format:

```
- YYYY-MM-DD · asked by: <who / where> · "<their question, in their words>" · project: <slug> · material: <paths> · post: <slug or —>
```

Keep the question verbatim. It is the title angle for the post and the hook in the email.

This list is written with Dirck, by hand. The weekly generator never reads it, and
nothing here goes into `blog-queue.md`.
